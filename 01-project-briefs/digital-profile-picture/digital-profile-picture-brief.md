## Part 1: Project Brief

### 1. Client Brief

* **Context:** A passport or identity card is proof of who we are, but the shift from physical documents to digital identity verification (such as digital passport photos) introduces new vulnerabilities.


* **Core Problem:** With the rise of artificial intelligence and image editing, it is becoming increasingly easy to subtly change or falsify identity photos, making photo fraud harder to detect and threatening the integrity of official documents.


* **Objective:** Find smart solutions against fraud with digital passport photos to safeguard identity verification processes.


* **Scope:** Advanced digital detection mechanisms, cryptographic validation, AI-based image authentication tools, or secure capture workflows that prevent manipulation while keeping the process user-friendly.



### 2. Requirement

* **Target Improvements:**
* **Fraud detection:** Advanced detection mechanisms for subtle digital manipulations, deepfakes, or alterations in photos.


* **Security and reliability:** Secure end-to-end verification workflows that prevent tampering without slowing down legitimate users.


* **Compliance:** Align with strict privacy regulations, data protection laws, and official identity governance standards.




* **In-Scope Outputs:** Proof-of-concepts, AI detection algorithms, secure application frameworks, or verification toolkits aimed at preventing digital passport photo fraud.


* **Exclusions:**
* Basic image compression tools or manual inspection methods that lack automated fraud detection capabilities.


* Solutions that violate privacy laws, GDPR, or process sensitive biometric data without legal or structural authorization.





### 3. Investigation

* **Domain Exploration:** Analyze digital forensics, image tampering techniques, identity verification protocols, and government security compliance frameworks.
* **Data & Technical Inquiry:** Examine how computer vision, machine learning classifiers, cryptographic watermarking, and metadata analysis can detect unauthorized photo manipulation in real time.

### 4. Issue

* **Core Tensions:** Balancing the absolute necessity for high-security fraud prevention with the user-friendliness, accessibility, and privacy rights of citizens submitting digital identity documents.

### 5. Contribution

* **Student Output:** Develop an independent conceptual design, detection algorithm prototype, or secure workflow architecture that addresses the identification and prevention of digital passport photo fraud.

### 6. Review

* **Evaluation Criteria:** Assess proposed solutions on detection accuracy, false-positive rates, compliance with privacy standards, and integration feasibility into official verification systems.

### 7. Reflection

* **Process Analysis:** Critically evaluate how technological arms races between digital fraudsters and AI-driven detection systems unfold within public sector security frameworks.

### 8. Insight

* **Transferable Learning:** Extract broader principles regarding digital trust, adversarial machine learning, and security-critical verification systems in public administration.
