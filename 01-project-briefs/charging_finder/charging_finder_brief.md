
## Project Brief: Data-Driven Solutions for Smarter Finding and Planning of Loading Areas in Zero-Emission Zones

### 1. Client Brief

* **Context:** The rapid growth of electric vehicles, parcel deliveries, and flash-parcel services is creating significant pressure on urban spaces. Zero-emission vans face major hurdles in finding spaces for loading and unloading.


* **Core Problem:** Scarcity of loading zones leads to illegal parking, traffic congestion, intensive maneuvering, and unsafe situations for other road users, making city logistics inefficient and unsustainable.


* **Objective:** Find innovative solutions or actionable insights before parking becomes a bottleneck that hinders the growth of zero-emission city logistics.


* **Scope:** Solutions can range from digital planning tools, apps, and navigation systems to physical/spatial concepts, pricing models, or smart infrastructure that help drivers find loading spots and assist municipalities in smart zone management.



### 2. Requirement

* **Target Improvements:**
* Improve the findability of existing loading and delivery zones for delivery drivers.


* Enhance the efficiency of city logistics with fewer logistical bottlenecks.


* Provide actionable insights for local authorities to optimize public space usage and compliance in urban environments.




* **In-Scope Outputs:** Innovative concepts, digital applications, or proof-of-concepts aimed at smarter finding and planning of loading areas in zero-emission zones.


* **Exclusions:**
* Generic routing software that lacks connection to specific urban loading infrastructure.


* Ideas that do not comply with local spatial safety and parking regulations.


* Planning software focused exclusively on traditional passenger cars.


* Solutions that require high-risk investments in new physical infrastructure.





### 3. Investigation

* **Domain Exploration:** Analyze urban mobility constraints, municipal regulations for zero-emission zones, driver behavior patterns, and infrastructure limitations.
* **Data & Technical Inquiry:** Examine how spatial data, real-time availability tracking, and navigation interfaces can interact with municipal management tools.

### 4. Issue

* **Core Tensions:** Balancing the operational needs of commercial delivery drivers with municipal goals of reducing congestion, lowering emissions, and maintaining pedestrian safety in limited urban spaces.

### 5. Contribution

* **Student Output:** Develop an independent conceptual design, functional model, or strategic framework that addresses the finding, planning, or management of zero-emission loading zones according to the project parameters.

### 6. Review

* **Evaluation Criteria:** Assess the proposed solution against practical feasibility, regulatory compliance, scalability for city logistics, and tangible impact on reducing urban friction.

### 7. Reflection

* **Process Analysis:** Critically evaluate how well the proposed solution bridges the gap between commercial logistics efficiency and municipal urban planning constraints.

### 8. Insight

* **Transferable Learning:** Extract broader principles regarding how data-driven tools and digital infrastructure can resolve spatial conflicts in constrained urban environments.
