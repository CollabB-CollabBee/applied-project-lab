## Part 1: Project Brief

### 1. Client Brief

* **Context:** The shift toward hybrid working brings increased flexibility, but it also creates challenges regarding how companies manage office spaces, maintain team cohesion, and handle asynchronous collaboration.


* **Core Problem:** When employees work partly at home and partly in the office, organizations struggle with coordination, maintaining corporate culture, avoiding isolation, and optimizing office space utilization.


* **Objective:** Find creative ideas to address the challenges associated with hybrid working.


* **Scope:** Solutions can range from digital workplace tools, scheduling apps, and virtual collaboration systems to office design concepts, management strategies, or cultural frameworks that make hybrid working successful.



### 2. Requirement

* **Target Improvements:**
* Improve office space utilization and prevent both overcrowding and empty desks.


* Enhance communication, teamwork, and company culture across distributed environments.


* Support managers and employees in navigating flexible schedules without administrative overload.




* **In-Scope Outputs:** Concepts, digital tools, workspace management systems, or organizational strategies focused on optimizing hybrid work environments.


* **Exclusions:**
* Rigid mandates that force employees back to full-time office work without flexibility.


* Generic enterprise software that lacks specific alignment with hybrid team culture and workspace coordination.





### 3. Investigation

* **Domain Exploration:** Analyze workplace psychology, hybrid team dynamics, office capacity management, and the friction points of mixed remote-office schedules.
* **Data & Technical Inquiry:** Examine how scheduling platforms, occupancy tracking, and asynchronous communication tools can streamline coordination without feeling intrusive.

### 4. Issue

* **Core Tensions:** Balancing the employee desire for absolute location flexibility with the organizational need for structural predictability, social cohesion, and efficient office space management.

### 5. Contribution

* **Student Output:** Develop an independent conceptual design, management strategy, or digital tool prototype that addresses the operational and cultural friction points of hybrid working.

### 6. Review

* **Evaluation Criteria:** Assess proposed solutions on feasibility, user adoption for both staff and management, positive impact on team culture, and scalability.

### 7. Reflection

* **Process Analysis:** Critically evaluate how organizations can redesign workflow infrastructure and culture for a permanent hybrid reality.

### 8. Insight

* **Transferable Learning:** Extract broader principles regarding modern workforce organization, digital-physical integration in office environments, and employee engagement models.
